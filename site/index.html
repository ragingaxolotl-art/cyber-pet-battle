Reflection and absorption require independently launched projectiles. Independent projectiles clash when both attacks enable clash durability. Each new contact spends one durability; zero destroys the projectile. Melee attacks clash only when both enable it: equal priority stops both, higher priority stops the weaker attack. Stopped attacks lose later hits and enter recovery.
